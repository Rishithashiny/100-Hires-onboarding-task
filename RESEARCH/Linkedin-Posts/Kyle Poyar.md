## Post 1: Service-as-a-Software — The Future of SaaS
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kyle-poyar_ai-startup-serviceasasoftware-activity-7285294782287937536-b7Jb
### Summary
Kyle explains how AI is transforming traditional Software-as-a-Service into what he calls "Service-as-a-Software." Instead of simply selling software access, modern SaaS companies increasingly deliver completed outcomes and business results using automation, AI, and human-in-the-loop workflows.
### Key Insights
* Services are becoming a competitive advantage rather than a weakness.
* Successful AI-native companies focus on specific workflows rather than broad platforms.
* Customers increasingly buy outcomes instead of software access.
* Pricing models are shifting from seat-based subscriptions to performance-based pricing.
* Future SaaS companies will compete against employees and service providers, not just software vendors.
### Application to B2B SaaS
Founders should focus on solving customer work rather than selling software features. The next generation of SaaS companies will likely win by combining AI, automation, and operational execution to deliver measurable business outcomes.

## Post 2: Who Owns Growth in a SaaS Company?
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kyle-poyar_saas-growth-operations-activity-7105174403629338624-xBp6
### Summary
Kyle examines one of the most important organizational questions in SaaS: who should own growth? Drawing on data from over 1,000 companies, he explores how Product, Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, and Growth teams contribute to revenue generation.
### Key Insights
* Growth is inherently cross-functional.
* Product-Led Growth companies often require different organizational structures.
* Traditional departmental ownership creates friction.
* Customer acquisition, activation, retention, and expansion must work together.
* Future SaaS organizations may require new leadership structures focused on growth rather than departments.
### Application to B2B SaaS
As SaaS companies scale, growth becomes too important to be isolated within a single department. Leaders should design systems that align Product, Marketing, Sales, and Customer Success around customer outcomes and revenue expansion.

## Post 3: Growth KPI Ownership in SaaS Organizations
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kyle-poyar_saas-growth-plg-activity-7096835825954377728-4gpE
### Summary
Kyle analyzes survey data from more than 1,000 SaaS companies to understand how growth-related KPIs are distributed across Marketing, Product, Growth, Sales, and Customer Experience teams. The findings reveal that growth ownership is often fragmented, especially in companies transitioning toward Product-Led Growth.
### Key Insights
* Marketing typically owns new sign-ups and lead generation.
* Growth teams influence multiple stages of the funnel but rarely own a single KPI outright.
* Product teams often own activation and self-serve revenue in PLG companies.
* Sales remains the primary owner of sales-assisted revenue.
* Customer Experience teams typically own retention and onboarding metrics.
* Many non-PLG companies underutilize Product as a growth function.
* Growth teams frequently face accountability without direct ownership authority.
### Application to B2B SaaS
As SaaS businesses scale, growth becomes increasingly cross-functional. Founders should create clear KPI ownership models while ensuring Product, Marketing, Sales, Customer Success, and Growth teams remain aligned around shared revenue objectives. The strongest growth organizations treat growth as a company-wide responsibility rather than a departmental function.-
