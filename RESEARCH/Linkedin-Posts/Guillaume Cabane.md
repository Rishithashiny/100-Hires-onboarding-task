## Post 1: The Hidden Playbooks of Elite B2B Growth Teams
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cabane_b2bmarketing-gtm-growthhacking-activity-7434679225607172096-qbbs
### Summary
Guillaume explores the unconventional tactics used by elite B2B growth teams that rarely get discussed publicly. He argues that the best growth operators look beyond standard playbooks and study human behavior, reciprocity, automation, and emerging market dynamics to gain competitive advantages.
### Key Insights
* Top-performing GTM teams often think differently from the broader market.
* Human attention becomes more valuable as AI-generated noise increases.
* Automation and human interaction should complement each other.
* Growth engineers frequently outperform traditional marketers in modern GTM environments.
* Competitive advantages often emerge from unconventional thinking rather than best practices.
### Application to B2B SaaS
Founders should avoid blindly copying industry playbooks. Sustainable growth often comes from identifying overlooked opportunities, experimenting aggressively, and combining automation with genuine human engagement.

## Post 2: Why People Buy From People in B2B
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cabane_ai-growth-growthmarketing-activity-7070015007924604928-DfyT
### Summary
Using the analogy of a handwritten letter, Guillaume explains why personalization and human connection remain powerful growth drivers even in highly automated B2B environments. He argues that curiosity, reciprocity, and authenticity significantly influence buying behavior.
### Key Insights
* Human psychology remains one of the strongest growth levers.
* Personalization works because it creates curiosity and reciprocity.
* Buyers respond more positively to communication that feels human.
* Proprietary data becomes increasingly valuable in an AI-driven world.
* AI should amplify personalization rather than replace human connection.
### Application to B2B SaaS
Growth teams should use technology to create more relevant and personalized experiences instead of producing more generic outreach. The companies that combine data, AI, and authentic human engagement will outperform purely automated competitors.

## Post 3: Why Most Founders Hire the Wrong Head of Growth
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/cabane_most-founders-hire-the-wrong-head-of-growth-activity-7379517677154902017-Avd9
### Summary
Guillaume challenges traditional hiring criteria for growth leaders. He argues that founders often prioritize polished resumes and past successes instead of identifying candidates who combine creativity, experimentation, and scientific thinking.
### Key Insights
* Growth is fundamentally an experimental discipline.
* The best growth leaders accept failure as part of the process.
* Creativity and analytical rigor must coexist.
* Past success does not guarantee future innovation.
* Effective growth teams learn rapidly through testing and iteration.
### Application to B2B SaaS
Founders should hire growth leaders who demonstrate curiosity, experimentation, and adaptability rather than simply evaluating historical achievements. Sustainable growth often comes from testing unconventional ideas and learning faster than competitors.
