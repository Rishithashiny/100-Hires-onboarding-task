## Post 1: How AI Is Reshaping Software, Growth, and Work
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_ai-changed-my-work-in-a-way-im-still-trying-activity-7457059285903085568-ScpH
### Summary
Kevin explains how agentic AI has fundamentally changed the way he works, enabling him to build landing pages, automate reporting, create testing workflows, and develop internal tools. He argues that AI's impact extends beyond productivity and is reshaping software value, distribution channels, and the role of human expertise.
### Key Insights
* Enterprise software remains valuable because its moat extends beyond code.
* Integrations, compliance, support, security, and accountability remain difficult to replicate.
* Distribution is becoming increasingly challenging as platforms retain users.
* Product quality and execution speed are becoming more important competitive advantages.
* Human judgment becomes more valuable as AI-generated output increases.
* Strategic thinking and verification are emerging as key skills.
### Application to B2B SaaS
B2B SaaS companies should focus on strengthening product differentiation, accelerating execution cycles, and investing in human expertise that guides AI rather than simply automating existing workflows.

## Post 2: Why Enterprise Software Is More Resilient Than People Think
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_ive-been-thinking-a-lot-about-whether-ai-activity-7457400412145483776-YIdx
### Summary
Kevin challenges the idea that AI will make software companies obsolete. He argues that software products are much more than code and that enterprise software companies possess durable advantages through integrations, governance, accountability, compliance, and operational reliability.
### Key Insights
* Software value extends far beyond application code.
* Enterprise customers prioritize accountability and operational stability.
* AI can replicate simple software workflows but struggles to replace enterprise infrastructure.
* Data providers should focus on strengthening proprietary data advantages.
* Systems of record should leverage AI to improve intelligence and usability.
* Competitive advantage increasingly comes from ecosystem strength rather than interface design.
### Application to B2B SaaS
SaaS companies should identify which aspects of their value proposition are defensible against AI disruption and double down on areas such as proprietary data, integrations, workflow ownership, and enterprise trust.

## Post 3: Five Growth Predictions for the AI Era
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kevinindig_in-my-final-growth-memo-of-2025-i-reflected-activity-7406417643831885824-yDrK
### Summary
Kevin shares five observations about the future of growth, SEO, and digital marketing. He highlights growing uncertainty in measurement, increasing complexity in AI-driven search environments, and the need for organizations to move beyond channel-based thinking toward integrated growth teams.
### Key Insights
* Traditional attribution models are becoming less reliable.
* AI-generated search experiences introduce measurement uncertainty.
* Many AI initiatives are currently driven by competitive pressure and fear of missing out.
* Growth professionals are increasingly becoming builders and operators.
* Future growth teams will integrate brand, PR, SEO, and product expertise.
* Outcomes matter more than channel ownership.
### Application to B2B SaaS
Organizations should build cross-functional growth teams that combine technical execution, content, brand, and product expertise while developing new approaches to measurement in an AI-driven search ecosystem.
