## Post 1: Rebuilding GTM Around AI
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kieranjflanagan_im-officially-no-longer-a-marketer-and-activity-7455968776593801216-4Hwm
### Summary
Kieran explains how AI has evolved from a supporting technology into the foundation of modern go-to-market strategy. After leading AI initiatives across HubSpot, he argues that companies should stop integrating AI into existing GTM processes and instead redesign their entire GTM around AI capabilities.
### Key Insights
* AI can automate large portions of customer acquisition and support workflows.
* Traditional marketing roles are evolving into cross-functional AI operating roles.
* AI-driven systems can significantly increase lead generation, win rates, and operational efficiency.
* Organizations need dedicated teams focused on AI experimentation and implementation.
* Competitive advantage comes from rapidly testing and operationalizing AI learnings.
### Application to B2B SaaS
Growth teams should rethink how marketing, sales, operations, and customer success work together. Rather than adding AI tools incrementally, companies should redesign workflows around AI-first execution and automation.

## Post 2: The AI-Mature Marketing Playbook
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kieranjflanagan_its-estimated-that-revenue-growth-for-ai-mature-activity-7394374643568238593-49_d
### Summary
Kieran outlines a future marketing playbook designed for AI-driven markets. He highlights major shifts in content consumption, search behavior, advertising economics, prospecting, and buyer engagement that will reshape B2B marketing strategies.
### Key Insights
* Creator-led content is becoming more effective than traditional brand content.
* Search is shifting from Google results to AI-generated answers.
* AI Engine Optimization (AEO) is becoming a critical growth discipline.
* Data quality is increasingly important for paid advertising performance.
* AI-powered prospecting requires prompt optimization and workflow refinement.
* AI sales agents may become the first interaction between buyers and vendors.
### Application to B2B SaaS
Companies should invest in AI search visibility, creator-driven content, AI-powered prospecting, and customer data infrastructure to remain competitive as traditional acquisition channels become less effective.

## Post 3: Optimize for Customers, Not Clicks
### Link
https://www.linkedin.com/posts/kieranjflanagan_ai-broke-your-marketing-because-youve-been-activity-7401983905991438336-3OTh
### Summary
Kieran argues that AI is exposing weaknesses in traditional performance marketing. As click-based optimization becomes less reliable, companies must refocus on understanding customers and use AI to continuously refine customer insights.
### Key Insights
* Performance marketing often prioritizes metrics over customer understanding.
* AI enables continuous updating of customer personas using real customer interactions.
* Customer research should be integrated into daily business operations.
* AI can synthesize insights from sales calls, support conversations, and customer feedback.
* Dynamic customer intelligence improves both marketing and sales effectiveness.
### Application to B2B SaaS
Growth teams should build AI-powered customer intelligence systems that continuously update buyer personas and customer journey insights using real-world customer data.
